# Delivery policy

## Current rules

Orders up to 2999 UAH are delivered with a paid shipping fee of 149 UAH. Orders from 3000 UAH and above are delivered free of charge. Delivery takes 2-5 business days within the city and 5-8 business days to the region. If the customer chooses courier delivery, the package is tracked in the system.

## Exceptions

Gift certificates and pre-orders are not covered by free delivery. Bulk orders from legal entities may have special terms.
